What changes, and when Standards with a dated change ahead of or behind them

Australia mirrors the international text, then adds to it. The AASB issues each IFRS Accounting Standard as an Australian equivalent with the same number — IFRS 15 becomes AASB 15. Standards inherited from the old IASC keep their IAS number internationally but are renumbered in the 100 series here, so IAS 1 is AASB 101 and IAS 41 is AASB 141.

On top of that sit the AASB 10xx standards, which have no international equivalent at all. They are where Australian practice genuinely diverges: not-for-profit income, the reporting tiers, public sector reporting and the additional disclosures the AASB requires.
